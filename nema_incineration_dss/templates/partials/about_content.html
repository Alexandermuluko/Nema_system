About the NEMA Incineration Decision Support System

This system applies machine-learning methods to historical hazardous-waste incineration data to provide decision-support predictions and model evaluation.

Workflow

Upload a CSV or Excel dataset, inspect and preprocess the data, select a verified target and input features, review prediction results, visualize trends, and generate a report.

Important scope

Predictions are advisory. This application does not provide real-time plant monitoring and does not automatically control incineration equipment. Confirm process-tag meanings and operational decisions with qualified personnel.
